Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 Street View Date Finder

1. The Short Version

We believe in privacy by default. You do not need an account, email address, or login to use Street View Date Finder. The tool runs directly in your browser, and we never collect, store, or sell your personal information or search queries.

2. How Search Data Works

When you paste a Street View link or coordinates into our tool:

  • Your browser sends requests directly to Google's public servers to check the photo timestamps. We do not intermediate or log your searches on any private database.
  • If you use the "Recent Searches" feature, that history is saved solely on your own computer using your browser's local storage (localStorage). It is never sent to our servers, and you can wipe it anytime by clicking "Clear".

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

To help pay for hosting and keep this tool free for everyone, we use Google AdSense to show ads.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the web.
  • You can turn off personalized advertising anytime by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party advertising cookies through the Network Advertising Initiative (www.aboutads.info).

4. Web Hosting Logs

Like any website on the internet, our hosting provider automatically records standard technical connection logs (such as your IP address, browser type, and the time of your visit). These logs are standard for preventing server abuse, blocking DDoS attacks, and keeping the website online.

5. Questions or Feedback

If you have questions about this policy or the website, you can reach out through our public GitHub repository:
