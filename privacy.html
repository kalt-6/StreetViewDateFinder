Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026 Street View Date Finder

1. Data Collection Practices

Our service requires minimal personal identification. Users can access all features freely without registering an account, providing an email, or logging into any profile.

Submitted Queries (Zero Persistence)
Submitted panorama identifiers or Google Maps URLs are evaluated directly within your browser runtime to query public interfaces. We never log, persist, or retain your queries, locations, or submitted links on external databases. Once a browser tab is dismissed, all temporary query data vanishes.

Diagnostic Logs & Hosting Metrics
Standard web hosting infrastructure logs general connection telemetry when requests arrive, such as IP addresses, browser specifications, operating system details, and visit timestamps. These logs are used purely for system security and network stability.

2. Advertising Partners (Google AdSense)

Display advertising through Google AdSense helps keep this tool accessible free of charge. Third-party providers, including Google, leverage browser cookies to serve relevant promotions based on earlier site visits across the web.

  • These advertising cookies allow network partners to deliver tailored promotional material informed by browsing activity.
  • Users can disable personalized ad tracking at any point via Google Ads Settings.
