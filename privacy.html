Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 Street View Date Finder (streetviewdate.com)

1. Commitment to User Privacy

Street View Date Finder ("we", "our", or "the Service") operates an online metadata utility located at streetviewdate.com. We are committed to transparency and safeguarding user privacy. Our tools operate entirely without mandatory account registration, logins, or tracking of personally identifiable information (PII).

2. Client-Side Execution & Query Data

When you submit a Google Maps URL, panorama ID, or geographic coordinate into our tool:

  • No Server Database Storage: Your inputs are processed directly within your browser runtime via client-side JavaScript that interfaces with Google's public RPC service.
  • No Query Tracking: We do not log, catalog, or associate your search locations or panorama requests with your IP address or identity.
  • Browser Local Storage: For user convenience, the tool provides an optional "Recent Extractions" feature that retains your last few searches locally on your device using HTML5 localStorage. This data never leaves your browser and can be deleted at any time by clicking "Clear History".

  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative (www.aboutads.info).

4. Web Server Logs & Telemetry

Like virtually all web hosts, the servers delivering our static assets (HTML, CSS, JS) automatically record standard technical log entries, which may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser user-agent string and operating system
  • Referring and exit pages
  • Timestamp of HTTP request

These server logs are used strictly for security monitoring, mitigating DDoS attacks, diagnosing CDN performance, and ensuring network reliability.

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have specific statutory rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including:

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have the right to:

  • Know what categories of personal information are collected and shared.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We do not sell personal information for monetary value. Advertising networks may utilize identifiers subject to opt-out rights via AboutAds.info.
